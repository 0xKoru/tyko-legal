2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact details (Sign in with Apple uses anonymised IDs)
  • We do not upload camera footage to any server
  • We do not sell or share your health data
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs

3. HealthKit

FitForm AI requests access to Apple HealthKit to read and write workout data. HealthKit data is only used within the app to power your fitness dashboard. It is never shared with third parties, used for advertising, or uploaded to any external server. You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in your iPhone Settings.

4. Location Data

Location access is only requested when you start an outdoor GPS workout. Your route is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can delete all route data from the app settings at any time.

5. Camera

Camera access is used exclusively for the AI Form Correction feature. Video analysis happens entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No video or image data leaves your device.

6. Sign in with Apple

We use Sign in with Apple for authentication. Apple provides us with an anonymised user identifier only. We do not receive your Apple ID email unless you choose to share it. We use this identifier solely to persist your premium subscription status.

7. In-App Purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not handle or store payment information. Purchase receipts are verified directly with Apple's servers.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

All your data lives on your device. To delete all data: Settings → FitForm AI → Delete All Data, or simply delete the app. Deleting the app removes all local data permanently.

9. Children's Privacy

FitForm AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe your child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features change. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
